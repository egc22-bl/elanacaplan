This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months. It then renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months. Renewal requires a written amendment signed by both parties at least sixty (60) days before expiry. There is no automatic renewal.
During the Term, Partner shall not market, refer, or resell a competing payments product to customers in the Territory. Exclusivity is limited to card-present acquiring and to customers Partner introduces after the Effective Date. Existing relationships and products outside acquiring are excluded.
Company shall pay Partner a referral fee equal to fifteen percent (15%) of net revenue actually collected from each Referred Customer for twenty-four (24) months after that customer’s first settled transaction. Company shall pay Partner a referral fee equal to twenty percent (20%) of net revenue for as long as each Referred Customer remains active, including revenue from products added after the first transaction.
Referral fees are calculated monthly and paid within forty-five (45) days after the end of the month in which the underlying revenue is collected. Referral fees are calculated weekly and paid within ten (10) business days. Refunds and chargebacks are trued up in the following period.
Company owns the customer relationship, the merchant agreement, and all customer data collected in connection with the services. Partner receives only the reports required to reconcile fees. Partner remains the primary relationship owner. Company may contact the customer only for underwriting, support of the payment service, and required regulatory notices, and must copy Partner on material communications.
Company may use transaction data to operate the service, meet legal obligations, and improve its products, including in aggregated or de-identified form. Company may use customer data solely to process transactions for that customer and to meet a legal obligation. Any use for product improvement, including aggregated use, requires Partner’s prior written consent.
Company will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain the service. Scheduled maintenance may occur at any time, with notice when practicable. Company will maintain 99.9% monthly uptime, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least five (5) business days in advance. Service credits apply if monthly uptime falls below that level.
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days’ prior written notice. Company may not terminate for convenience during the initial term. Partner may terminate for convenience on thirty (30) days’ notice. After termination, referral fees on then-active customers continue for twelve (12) months.
Each party’s aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. The liability cap does not apply to confidentiality breaches, data incidents, or indemnified third-party claims. For all other claims, the cap is the fees paid or payable in the twenty-four (24) months before the claim.
Each party will indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its gross negligence, willful misconduct, or material breach of this Agreement. Company will also indemnify Partner against third-party claims that the payment service infringes intellectual property rights, and against card-network fines caused by Company’s processing, except to the extent caused by Partner’s fraud or misuse.
Partner may, no more than once per calendar year and on thirty (30) days’ notice, audit fee calculations through an independent accountant bound by confidentiality. Partner may audit twice per calendar year, on ten (10) days’ notice, and may use its own finance team. Company will provide transaction-level detail sufficient to recalculate every fee.
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other’s prior written consent, except to an affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger or a sale of substantially all of its assets. Partner’s consent is required for any assignment by Company, including an assignment to a successor. A change of control of Company is treated as an assignment and gives Partner a right to terminate.
Neither party will issue a press release or use the other’s name or marks without prior written approval, except for a factual statement that a commercial relationship exists. Partner may list Company as a payments partner on its website and in sales materials, using marks Company has pre-approved, without a separate approval for each use.
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable in Ontario. The courts of Ontario have exclusive jurisdiction. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. Disputes are resolved by confidential binding arbitration in New York under the AAA Commercial Rules.
